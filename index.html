About Us

Creative work, backed by analysis

Not a designer who dabbles in data, or an analyst who dabbles in design — one practice built from both, from the start.

Most creative work stops at "does it look good." Drive goes further — every brand asset, dashboard and event is built to move a real number: attendance, engagement, conversions, retention.

That combination comes from an unusual path: eight years running live events end to end, alongside formal training in data science and business analytics. The result is a practice equally comfortable designing a brand identity, reading a customer dataset, or running the floor on event night.

Technical Projects — IoT & Data Engineering

Smart Monitoring & Irrigation System

Challenge

Caring for a bonsai by eye and guesswork often means watering too much or too little, with no easy way to know what's actually happening in the soil.

Solution

We built a small sensor system that watches the plant's soil, light and health around the clock, shows everything on a simple live dashboard, and waters it automatically the moment it needs water — no manual checking required.

Outcome

Round-the-clock visibility and hands-off watering, built cheaply enough to scale to more plants — and a foundation we can build on to predict watering needs ahead of time.

Technical Projects — Machine Learning

Predictive Maintenance for Aircraft Engines

Challenge

Unplanned aircraft engine failures are costly and dangerous — maintenance teams need advance warning before a part actually fails.

Solution

We trained a machine learning model on real aircraft engine sensor data to predict how much working life each engine has left, with the results fed into a live dashboard maintenance teams can actually check.

Outcome

A working early-warning system that flags engines needing attention before they fail, rather than after — the same approach for any physical asset.
